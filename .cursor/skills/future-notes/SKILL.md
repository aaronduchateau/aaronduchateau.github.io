## ADA auto-queued animations

Full hybrid quiz (questions, then teach, then more questions) lives in:

[ada-autoqueued-animations.md](ada-autoqueued-animations.md)

**Target conclusion:** two back-to-back auto-queued animations **can** be ADA/WCAG 2.1 AA compliant, including a sequence longer than 5 seconds, **if** there is an **accessible dismiss (hide)** that actually stops the motion.

Do not treat an undocumented pause between clips as a legal reset of the 5-second clock.
